Welcome to the Retrieval prototype

Try the whole retrieval process, from creating a ticket to closing it, and break it on purpose to see how it copes.

This is a working prototype of how the Asset Team gets equipment back from a team member who is leaving, recalled or absent. You can do everything a real operator would do, from creating a ticket to closing it. Nothing is really sent, nobody is really emailed, and anything you change stays in your own browser.

20Email templates for every retrieval scenario
5Retrieval forms that team members sign
14Stages from checklist to closure
12Guided missions to follow
💡

The big idea: Fill in the case once when you create a ticket. Every email and form for that case uses the same values. No copy and pasting, no mismatched names, no forgotten fields.

Four things to know before you start

  1. Nothing leaves this page

    Nothing leaves this page. Emails, texts and notices to IT are recorded as if sent, so you can see what would happen.

  2. Your work stays in your browser

    Your work is saved in this browser only. Another person opening the link sees their own copy, and clearing your browser data starts you fresh.

  3. You cannot break it

    You cannot break it. If a ticket gets into a state you do not like, use Clear All Tickets in the flask menu at the top of the screen and start again.

  4. Two sets of controls

    Two sets of controls let you create situations on demand: the dark Prototype controls bar, and the flask icon in the top bar. Both are explained under Controls.

The controls

Two sets of controls create situations that would take days to happen for real. They are for this demo only.

The system around you is a prototype, so it comes with tools to test it quickly. You will find them in two places. Try both: they are the fastest way to see how the system copes with problems.

1. The Prototype controls bar

The dark strip across the very top of the app. Four dropdowns change how the screens behave. If it is hidden, switch it back on in the flask menu.

State

Shows how the screen behaves when things are slow, missing or broken.

👉

Try this: Switch to Loading to see the loading screens, Not found to see what a missing ticket looks like, and Error to see the error page. Switch back to Populated to carry on.

Viewing as

Chooses which Asset Team member you are acting as: D. Reyes, J. Santos or M. Bautista.

👉

Try this: Switch person, then turn on Assigned to me in the Ticket Tracker. Open a ticket that belongs to someone else and take it over from the menu at the top right of the ticket.

Scenario

Changes how much data there is.

👉

Try this: First-timer, no tickets shows the empty list a brand new user sees. High volume fills the list with about 35 tickets so you can try paging and the Show 10, 15 or 25 control.

Situation

Creates a problem in the data on purpose.

👉

Try this: Choose No contact number, then open a ticket and look at the Custodian card. Try to confirm contact details and see how the system guides you to fix it.

2. The flask menu (Simulation Control)

The flask icon in the top bar, next to the sun and moon button and the bell. It makes things happen to your tickets, the way the outside world would.

Push Simulated Update

Pretends the employee record changed a team member's Last Working Day. Optionally pick the ticket and the new date.

👉

Try this: Push an update, then open that ticket. A banner says the Last Working Day changed, the Target Date moves, and the IT Access Lock card says IT needs the new date.

Push Simulated Reply

Stands in for the team member answering an email.

👉

Try this: First create a remote ticket assigned to you, choose Email reply at Branch confirmation and send the request. Then press Push Simulated Reply: a reply notification arrives and the ticket is flagged. None of the five sample tickets is waiting for a reply.

Notification types

Sends any kind of notification to the bell, or one of every kind at once.

👉

Try this: Choose Push One of Every Type, then open the bell at the top. Unread items come first, and each one opens the ticket it is about. Some items are dated a few days back, so look under Today, This week and This month.

Morning digest

Overdue, due soon, checklist, shipment and quotation alerts are bundled into one email at 8:00 AM. Everything else is emailed at once.

👉

Try this: First press Push One of Every Type, so there is something waiting. Then press Send Digest Now, then View last digest to read what would have been emailed. With nothing waiting it only says "Nothing waiting".

Run Alert Check

The system checks for overdue and due soon tickets every minute. This runs the check now.

👉

Try this: After changing a ticket's dates, run it and watch the bell.

Act as

Same idea as Viewing as: switch the Asset Team member.

👉

Try this: Create a ticket and assign it to someone else. Switch to that person and look for the notification.

Allow sending with unresolved placeholders

Normally the system refuses to send an email that still has a blank placeholder such as a missing date. This lets it through.

👉

Try this: Leave it off to see the refusal and the Placeholder Check. Turn it on when you want to push a ticket through quickly.

Fail the next email send

The mail server refuses the next send, once.

👉

Try this: Turn it on, press Send on any notice, and read the Email not sent banner. Press the same button again to retry. Nothing was recorded by the failed attempt.

Show the prototype controls bar

Shows or hides the dark strip at the very top of the app.

👉

Try this: Hide it for a clean look at the real screens. Bring it back here any time.

Clear All Tickets

After you confirm, deletes every ticket (the five samples too) and every notification, leaving an empty list. Reloading the page should bring the sample tickets back.

👉

Try this: Use it when the system says a team member already has an open ticket, or whenever you want a clean slate.

⚠

Stuck? If you cannot create a ticket because the team member already has one, open the flask menu and press Clear All Tickets. It asks you to confirm, then removes every ticket and notification. Reload the page to get the sample tickets back.

Guided missions

12 short tasks that walk the whole retrieval process. Do them in any order and tick the steps as you go.

Each mission takes about five minutes. Together they cover creating a ticket, every stage through closure, the courier route for remote tickets, charging, and what happens when things go wrong.

✅

Your ticks are saved in this browser. The bar at the very top of the page shows how much of the guide you have read.

4Work the first three stages

Checklist, IT notice and asset confirmation: the setup before anything is sent. About 6 minutes.

  • Open the ticket you just created.

  • On asset confirmation, press Show Custodian card, tick the assets, then Confirm Assets.

  • Locking access is IT's job. The stage tells IT and never waits for them.
  • The Custodian card pulses to show where it is.
  • After Confirm Assets the list locks and the ticket gets a Target Date.

The main email to the team member, and every way a send can go. About 8 minutes.

  • Open the flask menu and turn on Fail the next email send. Press Send and read the Email not sent banner. Press the same button to retry.

8Receive the equipment, charge if needed, then close

The last third of a ticket, including the money side. About 10 minutes.

  • On the Overdue stage (ticket 423 is already there), try Send Overdue Reminder (only when overdue) and Mark Equipment Received.
  • Try Equipment won't be returned? to see the charging case open straight away.
  • On Receipt and evidence, add a Proof-of-Receipt file and Condition Photos, then confirm.
  • On Post-Retrieval Charging, choose Not Applicable, or Item Damaged or Not Returned. Work through the six steps of Case Details and pick an outcome.
  • On Returned Item Check and routing, pick Standard, Condition or Completeness Issue, or Suspected Tampering or Loss, then press Confirm Inspection & Routing. A Settlement Election skips this stage.
  • On Closure, read Before you close, then press Review & Close Ticket. Set each item to Returned, Send to charging or Keep Open.
  • In the Close Ticket window tick "Send the team member a detailed closure copy", press "View contents to be sent", use Copy or Download EML, then confirm.
👁

What to look for

  • There are four charging outcomes: No Charge, Quotation Pending, Determination & Charge, and Settlement Election. Each has its own email, and all except No Charge also have a form.
  • The system does not post deductions. It records a handoff to Payroll.
  • An item already in a charging case defaults to In charging case when you close.
  • The Before you close checks are advice. None of them stops you.

Ticket Tracker

Your operational home base. Each ticket is one retrieval case, from first notice to final closure.

The ticket list

  • The headline tells you how many tickets need attention, with how many of the open tickets are yours underneath.
  • Three chips (Open, Due soon, Overdue) show counts and filter the table. Hover one for the breakdown by Onsite, Remote and Mine. Press again to clear.
  • Search and filters sit in one row: search by name or ticket number, status chips, Assigned to me, Type and Deployment.
  • The table is as tall as its rows. The page header stays put and everything beneath it scrolls. The first five columns stay in place when you scroll sideways. Show 10, 15 or 25 per page.
  • Return Deadline shows a countdown: orange within 3 days, red when overdue. Closed tickets have none.
  • Export selected downloads the ticked tickets as a spreadsheet file.

Creating a ticket

  1. Choose the team member

    Press Create Ticket, then Retrieval. Find Team Member shows employee number and email. Everything below stays locked until you pick one.

  2. Check what filled itself in

    The reason, deployment type (Onsite or Remote), Last Working Day and Separation Date come from the employee record. Change the reason or deployment type and the form asks for a short note explaining why.

  3. Add what the reason needs

    Recalled needs a Recall Effective Date. Paths with approval gates need the authorizing endorsement. Unexplained Absence forces Remote.

  4. Choose the assignee

    Each option shows a full name with employee number and email. The Summary card shows the deadline, how it was worked out, and which email will go out.

  5. Create the ticket

    A team member who already has an open ticket is blocked, with a link to that ticket.

Inside a ticket

Header

Ticket number, Open or Closed, the reason, deployment type and who it is assigned to. Gate Passes, Close Ticket and a menu with Copy Ticket Link, Artifacts Summary, Activity Log, Change reason or mode, Take over ticket and Cancel retrieval.

Phase stepper

Six phases on a remote ticket, four on an onsite one (Branch & Endorsement and Transit are skipped). Press a reached step to revisit it. A future step says it has not been reached yet. On a phone it becomes a stage menu.

Stage card

The step you are on, with its one main button. A button that is not ready stays clickable and explains why. After a send, a banner confirms it and you press Continue.

Right panel

Custodian (with contact details to verify), Assets Confirmed, Target Date, Communications (texts), Courier and Shipping (remote), and IT Access Lock. Press the panel icon at the top right of the ticket header, next to the three dots menu (or the ] key) to hide it. It shows on large screens only.

Activity Log

Every action with who did it and when, shown in your own time zone. Open it from the three dots menu.

Banners

Above the stage card: a changed Last Working Day (press Acknowledge), a cancelled ticket, a delayed shipment and a failed send.

Changing course

Take over a ticket

Moves a ticket to you. A handover note is required, and the previous assignee is notified. The option is dimmed on a ticket you already own and tells you why when pressed.

Change reason or deployment type

Allowed until a notice is sent, a call is logged or an acknowledgment is recorded. After that it is locked and says which of those locked it. A note is required, and the deadline and email are worked out again.

Cancel retrieval

For a resignation that was withdrawn or a recall that was reversed. Choose a reason and write a note. The ticket becomes Cancelled and stops blocking a new ticket for the same person.

Reopen

Any closed ticket, including a cancelled one, can be reopened from the header. It returns to open at the stage it was on.

Edit the Target Date

In the right panel. A reason is required and every change is kept in the date history. If the ticket was overdue, the log notes that the lapse remains on record.

How to edit and send a template

  1. Find your template

    Open the Templates page, search by name, filter by mode (Onsite or Remote) or status (Active, Draft, Retired), and open the template. Press Edit to change it.

  2. Edit the subject and body

    The rich text editor works like a word processor. Bold, italic, headings, lists, tables, and links are all supported.

  3. Insert placeholders

    Click the Placeholders button to open the drawer. Click a token to insert it at your cursor. In the ticket workspace tokens appear as colored chips: {{TM_FULL_NAME}} has data, {{ASSET_TAG}} is missing data. The Templates editor shows every placeholder in blue.

  4. Set CC and BCC

    Add recipients who should be copied. P&C and the team member's Leadership Coach are common CC recipients.

  5. Preview

    Watch the Sample preview pane beside the editor: it shows the email as the recipient would see it, with sample data filling in the placeholders.

  6. Copy or download

    Open More actions, then choose Copy to clipboard to paste into Outlook, or Download EML to save a .eml file for Outlook import.

Placeholder colors explained:

Blue = filled with data (the editor shows every placeholder in blue).

Orange = the ticket is missing this data. Go back to the ticket and fill in the field.

Red, struck through = unrecognized token. Check for typos in the placeholder code. Grey = a fixed company value.

Before sending: pre-flight checklist

  • All placeholders are green (no yellow or red tokens remaining).
  • CC and BCC recipients are set (P&C, Leadership Coach, supervisor as needed).
  • Subject line includes the team member's name and APR reference number.
  • Equipment table matches what is actually assigned (correct asset tags and serials).
  • The ticket's email preview confirms the email looks correct with the real data.

How Templates, Forms, and Tickets Connect

Three modules, one source of truth. Here is how data flows between them.

Data flows from the ticket into both the email template and the attached form. Fill in once, use everywhere.
💡

Aha! moment: You never copy-paste a team member's name, employee ID, or asset tag into an email. The ticket holds all the case data, and every template and form reads from it. Change a date in the ticket, and every email and form for that case updates automatically.

The connection in 5 points

  1. Templates hold the email content with {{PLACEHOLDER}} tokens (e.g. {{TM_FULL_NAME}}, {{ASSET_TAG}}).
  2. Forms are linked to templates as attachments (e.g. the "Resignation (Remote)" template attaches the "Equipment Return Acknowledgment (Remote)" form).
  3. Tickets hold the real case data (team member info, equipment, dates, courier details).
  4. When you open a ticket, the system automatically matches the right template based on the ticket's reason and mode.
  5. The form attached to that template also gets its placeholders filled from the same ticket data.
✨

One source of truth: Edit a template once in the Templates section, and every ticket using that template gets the updated version. Fill in case data once in a ticket, and every email and form for that case reflects the same values.

Typical Workflow

A real example: processing a resignation (remote) from start to finish.

📋

Scenario. Maria, an Asset Officer, hears that Juan dela Cruz has resigned. Juan works remotely and will ship his MacBook Pro back by courier.

  1. Create the ticket

    Ticket Tracker, Create Ticket, Retrieval. Maria picks Juan. The reason, deployment type and dates fill in. She reads the Summary card, which shows the deadline and the email that will be sent, and creates the ticket.

  2. Setup stages

    She works the offboarding checklist, presses Notify IT and continue, then confirms Juan's equipment on the Custodian card and presses Confirm Assets. The Target Date is set.

  3. Courier branch

    Because Juan is remote, Maria picks JRS or LBC and the branch nearest his address, captured by call or email. She then sends the courier endorsement email to the courier account manager.

  4. Send the notice

    She previews the email, checks the Placeholder Check is clear, and presses Send. If she sent it from her own Outlook instead, she would press Mark as sent outside the system.

  5. Follow up

    She logs a call. If Juan does not answer three times she copies the follow up text from the right panel, sends it from her phone and marks it sent. The midpoint and final reminders are ready for her to send or skip.

  6. In transit

    Juan ships the laptop. Maria enters the tracking number on Confirm In Transit. The overdue clock stops.

  7. Receipt and evidence

    The laptop arrives. Maria uploads the Proof-of-Receipt and Condition Photos and confirms the evidence is complete.

  8. Charging and check

    The screen is cracked. In Post-Retrieval Charging she chooses Item Damaged or Not Returned, works through Case Details and chooses an outcome. The system sends the email and form, and she marks it referred to Payroll. She then routes the item at the Returned Item Check.

  9. Close

    She reads Before you close, sets the laptop to In charging case, sends Juan the closure copy and closes the ticket.

💬

The same flow applies to every reason. Only the email, form and deadline change. Try it yourself with mission 3, then missions 4 to 7.

Reasons and deadlines

The reason a team member is leaving decides the deadline, the email and whether there are approval gates.

ReasonGatesDeadline
ResignationNoneLast Working Day for onsite, plus 3 days for remote
Immediate ResignationNoneThe day the ticket is created, plus 3 days for remote
End of Contract1Last Working Day, plus 3 days for remote
Recalled1Recall Effective Date, plus 3 days for remote
Unexplained Absence3Remote only, Last Working Day plus 3 days
ℹ

Gates are approvals by P&C. They are advice and never stop a stage or a send. Days are calendar days. Overdue means the Target Date has passed: the due date itself is not overdue, and confirming In Transit stops the clock.

Troubleshooting

Common questions and issues, answered where they happen.

Placeholder showing as orange?

An orange placeholder (in the ticket workspace) means the ticket is missing data for that field. Open the Placeholder Check with the icon on the attached email or form, press the entry and fill in the value. Common culprits are a missing Target Date, an acknowledgment due date, or an asset with no serial number or tag.

Placeholder showing as red?

A red, struck through placeholder is a code the system does not recognize, usually a typo such as {{TM_NAME}} instead of {{TM_FULL_NAME}}. Check the spelling against the Placeholders drawer in the template editor.

"Resolve placeholders before sending" and I want to carry on

Open the flask menu and turn on Allow sending with unresolved placeholders. Turn it off again to see the real guard.

"Email not sent"

The mail server refused the message, nothing was recorded, and the same Send button retries it. In this demo that happens only when you turn on Fail the next email send in the flask menu.

A team member already has an open ticket

Only one open retrieval ticket is allowed per person. Open the existing ticket from the link, cancel it, or press Clear All Tickets in the flask menu.

A button looks grey

Buttons that are not ready are never really disabled. Press one and a message says what to do first.

A ticket shows as overdue

The Target Date has passed and the equipment is not back. The overdue stage offers the overdue reminder. Confirming In Transit stops the clock, and changing the Target Date with a reason clears it.

The wrong email is attached

Emails are chosen from the reason and deployment type. Check them on the ticket, or replace the attached email on the notice stage. Reason and deployment type lock once a notice is sent.

The reason or deployment type will not change

It is locked because a notice was sent, a call was logged, an acknowledgment was recorded, or the ticket is closed. The message names which.

I lost my progress or my tickets

Tickets and ticks are saved in this browser only. A different browser, a private window or cleared site data starts fresh with the sample tickets.

The prototype controls bar is missing

Open the flask menu and turn on Show the prototype controls bar.

Formatting looks wrong after pasting into Outlook

Use Paste as HTML, or press Download EML on the email preview and open the file in Outlook, which keeps all formatting.

Cannot find the charging emails

They appear in Post-Retrieval Charging after you choose Item Damaged or Not Returned, or when you press Equipment won't be returned? on the awaiting return stage.

Questions about the demo

Does anything really get sent?

No. Emails, texts and notices to IT are recorded as sent so you can follow the flow. Use Download EML if you want to see the real email file.

Where is my data kept?

In this browser only. Another browser, another device or a private window starts with the sample tickets.

A team member already has an open ticket and I cannot create another.

Open the flask menu and press Clear All Tickets (it asks you to confirm), or cancel the existing ticket.

A button looks grey. Is it broken?

No. Buttons that are not ready stay clickable and tell you why with a message.

The send is refused because of a placeholder.

Use the Placeholder Check (the icon on the attached email or form) to see which one, or turn on Allow sending with unresolved placeholders in the flask menu.

Where is the flask icon?

In the top bar, next to the sun and moon button and the bell. The dark strip across the very top is the Prototype controls bar, and the same flask menu can show or hide it.

Can I use it on a phone?

Yes. Phone, tablet and desktop are all designed. On a phone the stage steps turn into a menu.

Glossary

Quick definitions for the terms you will see throughout the system.

APR

Asset Processing Request. The ticket reference number, formatted like APR-26-00422. Every retrieval case gets a unique one.

ATD

Authority to Deduct. The form that authorizes a payroll deduction for damaged, unreturned or kept equipment. Nothing is deducted until the team member signs it.

Return Ack

Equipment Return Acknowledgment. The form the team member signs to confirm they got the notice and will return the equipment.

Org Constants

Values that are the same for every ticket, such as the Asset Team email and the office address. They fill in automatically.

Retrieval ticket

One case of getting equipment back from one team member.

Onsite and Remote

Onsite means the team member returns equipment in person. Remote means they ship it through a courier branch.

Last Working Day

The last day of work. Most deadlines count from here.

Target Date

The date the equipment must be back. It is worked out from the reason and deployment type, and you can change it with a reason.

Overdue Return

The Target Date has passed and the equipment is not back. Confirming In Transit pauses this.

Placeholder

A blank in an email or form, written like {{TM_FULL_NAME}}, that the system fills from the ticket.

Gate

An approval step by P&C on certain paths. Gates are advice and never block you.

Custodian

The team member who holds the equipment.

Post-Retrieval Charging

The process for charging a team member when equipment is damaged or not returned.

Hold Off

The status after the equipment is received while it is checked.

EML

An email file. Download EML gives you the email as a file you can open and send from your own mail program.
