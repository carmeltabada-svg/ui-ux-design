Tutorial

Getting started

Open Assets in the left menu. The title tells you how many units are tracked, and the line under it says where they are. Below that, six small cards show the numbers that matter. Under them is the list.

Your role is read only. You can look at everything, filter, copy tags and serials, and open documents. The Move and Edit buttons do not appear for you.

First run, about two minutes:

  1. Click Status above the list. A menu opens with every status under its group, each with a count. The retired statuses sit in their own Legacy group at the end.
  2. Type a few letters to search, tick one or two statuses, then press Esc. The list narrows and a chip appears under the toolbar, such as "Status: In Use".
  3. Click All filters. A panel slides in with every filter and the number of assets that match. The button at the bottom reads "Show N assets".
  4. Remove the chip with its x, or press Clear all. The full list is back.
How to

Do the common things

Narrow the list with filters

The bar above the list has the filters you use most: Status Asset Type Asset Name Ownership Warranty Status. The rest are behind All filters.

  1. Open a filter and tick the values you want. Within one filter the values add up (In Use or Available). Between filters they combine (In Use and Brand Lenovo).
  2. Read the small number beside each option. It is how many assets you would get if you ticked it, with your other filters on. A zero means nothing would match.
  3. Asset Name is greyed out until you pick an Asset Type. Then it lists only the names of that type, such as Laptop or Monitor.
  4. Long values are cut with three dots. Hover to read the whole value.

Find where a unit is

The Location column shows an icon and a place. A team member's home address, a supplier or a client is plain text. An office is a blue link to its location page.

Move an asset

Press the truck icon at the end of a row, or Move asset in the header of an asset page. A menu asks which kind of move you want:

KindUse it to
Check In or Check OutMove the unit to any other status.
Return to supplierSend the unit back to its supplier.
Client pull-outRecord the client taking the unit back. It is greyed out for Monthly Rental units: hover it to read why.

The full steps are in the Move Asset guide.

Export a few assets
  1. Tick the rows you want. The count appears above the list, such as "2 selected".
  2. Press Export selected. A CSV file downloads with those rows.

If you filter so a ticked row leaves the list, it is no longer selected.

Read an asset page

Click a tag. The top shows the tag (with a copy button), the status, who has it and where, and the buttons for what you can do. The left card has the details; the right side has three cards. On a unit that was returned or pulled out, Move details opens a panel from the right with what was recorded, and any document attached to the move opens as the real file in a new tab.

Edit an asset's details
  1. Press Edit details. The page scrolls to the card, which gets a blue border and a banner.
  2. Change a field. Required fields are marked. Press Ctrl or Cmd + S to save, or Esc to cancel.
If a menu is open, Esc closes the menu first. Press Esc again to leave edit mode.

Explanation

Why it works this way

Why are the filters in one row?

There are eleven filters. Showing them all at once took more than half the screen before the list began. The five you use most stay on the row, and the rest are one click away.

Why do the cards not group by stage?

The Status filter already groups by stage. The cards show numbers it cannot, such as what is overdue or about to lose its warranty.

Why do some parts of a list or menu fade at the edge?

The fade is a hint that there is more to scroll to. It shows only on an edge that still has content past it, so there is none at the very top, none at the very bottom, and none on a list that fits.

Why can I only click offices?

Only offices have a page in the Locations menu. A home address, a supplier or a client do not, so they stay plain text.

Why does Total used age look a month higher than I expect?

Each person's time is counted in whole 30 day months, then added up. This is how the live system counts it.

Questions

Common questions

Nothing shows after I filter.

No asset matches every filter at once. Remove a chip, or press Clear all.

Asset Name is greyed out.

Pick an Asset Type first. The names depend on it.

A model or purchase order is blue but opens only a note.

Those pages already exist elsewhere in the system. In this release the link only tells you so.

The Warranty card says it cannot be calculated.

The unit has no purchase order. Press Link PO in the card (asset officers) to add one.

Custody history shows dashes.

The unit was never available or never deployed, so there is nothing to count yet.

Why is a movement missing a note or document?

It had none. The box stays and says "No note recorded" or "No document attached".
